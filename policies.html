01Shipping

Orders ship within 5 business days of purchase.

We ship to addresses in the United States for now. A flat shipping fee is shown at checkout before you pay.

When your order ships, you get an email with tracking. Delivery times depend on the carrier.

02Returns

Every piece is one of a kind, so every sale is final. We do not take returns for fit, change of mind or preference. Please read the size, measurements and condition notes before you buy.

The exception is a piece that arrives not as described: different from its listing in condition, size, measurements or photos. Email us within 7 days of delivery with your order and photos of the problem. If we agree the piece is not as described, we refund the full price and the original shipping, and we tell you how to send the piece back.

03Authenticity

Each piece is described by its seller. poda chooses which pieces appear in a folder, but we do not authenticate them. If a piece is not as described, see Returns above.

04How consignment works

Pieces come from private closets, independent brands and boutiques. poda presents them and sells them on the seller's behalf. When a piece sells, poda keeps a commission and the seller receives the rest.

05Payments

Prices are in US dollars. Payments are handled by Stripe. poda never sees or stores your card number.
