poda

poda is a curated resale and consignment store bringing together independent brands, vintage sellers, and private closets.

new selections drop every two weeks.

joining

poda works with a select group of sellers. we review each application to make sure it aligns with the store and its visual identity.

apply to be considered. if selected, you’ll receive a seller account.

what best describes you?

how you can sell

sell in a way that works for you.

private closet

  • a single piece from your closet
  • a selection of pieces you no longer wear
  • pieces you want to make available over time

vintage / resale store

  • one-off vintage finds
  • a selection from your current inventory
  • pieces sourced for upcoming drops

independent brand

  • pieces from your current collection
  • past-season or archive pieces
  • samples and one-offs

selection

submit pieces through your seller account. poda reviews submissions and selects pieces for each biweekly drop.

you can submit one piece or build a larger selection over time.

why poda

poda pairs each drop with editorial and imagery that give the pieces context.

we have a deep sourcing network and connect sellers with people looking for pieces that align with what they offer.

the drop

selected pieces are listed in the poda shop. when a piece sells, you handle shipment and receive your agreed payout.

pricing, commission, and fulfillment are agreed before a piece goes live.
