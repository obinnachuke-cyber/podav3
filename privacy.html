Effective November 20, 2026. This explains what poda collects when you use the site, subscribe or buy a piece.

01What we collect

Subscribing: your email address.

Buying: your name, email address, shipping address and the order itself. Your card details are entered with Stripe and never reach poda.

Selling with poda: what you put in an application or a seller account, including your contact details and photos of pieces.

Visiting: poda does not use advertising trackers. When you follow an Index link to Instagram we count the click, without your identity. Signed-in sellers have a session stored in their browser so they stay signed in.

02How we use it

To process and ship your order, send you the emails about it, answer your questions, run seller accounts and payouts, and keep the site secure. Subscribers get emails about drops, the Index and Market Notes. Every one of them has an unsubscribe link.

poda does not sell your personal information.

03Who handles it

poda uses a few services to run the site, and each sees only what its job needs.

Stripe takes payments and holds card details. Supabase stores accounts, orders and subscribers. Cloudflare serves the site. Resend sends our emails.

When you buy a piece from a seller, we give that seller your name and shipping address so they can send it to you. We may also share information when the law requires it.

04Your choices

Unsubscribe from any email with the link at the bottom of it. To see, correct or delete the information poda holds about you, email us. We keep order records as long as needed for accounting, tax and disputes.

We may update this page. The date at the top shows the latest version.
