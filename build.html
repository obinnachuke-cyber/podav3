Behind the transaction.

  • 01 / Seller InfrastructureBuilt

    Seller accounts, invitations, and database rules that keep each seller to their own inventory.

  • 02 / Inventory OperationsBuilt

    Sellers submit pieces, I review them, and approved pieces go live at a scheduled release time.

  • 03 / CommercePartial

    Stripe checkout for one piece at a time, with an inventory hold during payment; it is switched off in the current config and a multi-item cart is not built.

  • 04 / Financial LogicBuilt

    Commission rates are set for each supplier and saved on every order, while payouts are recorded by hand and not automated.

Built works in the repository. Partial is built but incomplete or switched off.

Built as a business. Engineered as a product.

Poda began with a simple question: how can independent sellers reach customers without managing their own storefronts?

I translated that question into a functioning product with defined user roles, operational workflows, and transaction economics.
