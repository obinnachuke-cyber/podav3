PODA CAPITAL — FULL PRODUCT / WEBSITE UPDATE BRIEFI want to substantially update the current Poda Capital website and product direction based on a clearer business model.Do not rebuild everything unnecessarily. Preserve the existing visual language, brand identity, typography, logo, and any parts of the current implementation that still make sense. The goal is to simplify the product around the new Poda model and remove features or positioning that make Poda feel like an overly complicated software platform.==================================================1. WHAT PODA IS==================================================Poda is a curated, relationship-driven clothing retailer built around access to interesting people's closets.The core idea:Interesting people have strong wardrobes and regularly own clothing they would potentially sell, but they often do not want to photograph, list, negotiate, ship, or actively manage resale themselves.Poda builds relationships with these people and gradually develops a network of closets.Closet owners can indicate which pieces they are currently willing to sell.Poda reviews this available inventory, selects only the strongest pieces, and merchandises them into curated drops.The public customer does NOT see every piece people submit.The public customer sees what Poda selected.The core loop is:CLOSET→ AVAILABLE→ PODA SELECTS→ DROP→ SOLDPoda is NOT trying to be:- another Grailed- another Depop- an open marketplace- a wardrobe tracking SaaS product- a giant resale database- a traditional consignment store that accepts everything- a fashion-news publication- a personal styling companyPoda should feel like:A merchant with access to great closets.The central value proposition is:"Poda selected this."Over time, the quality of Poda's selection should become the reason buyers return.==================================================2. DAY-ONE BUSINESS MODEL==================================================Keep the initial product intentionally small.There are only three major parts of Poda right now:1. DROPS2. CLOSETS / CONSIGNMENT3. MARKET NOTESDo NOT build:- sourcing services- AI valuations- automated payouts- advanced seller analytics- brand portals- social networking- messaging systems- complicated marketplace functionality- native mobile applications- memberships- wholesale infrastructure- seller storefronts- extensive SaaS dashboardsThose may eventually exist, but they are not part of the current MVP.==================================================3. HOW THE CLOSET NETWORK WORKS==================================================Poda begins with a small invite-based network of closet owners.Initially this may only be:- 3–8 closet owners- each with approximately 5–15 items documented- potentially only 30–80 total pieces in the private inventory poolThese people might include:- serious fashion consumers- collectors- stylists- creative professionals- artists / DJs / music-industry people- fashion-industry employees- people with strong contemporary luxury wardrobes- people with vintage/archive collectionsThe quality of the closet matters much more than the number of users.Poda should not publicly position itself as a mass-market marketplace where anyone can upload anything.Closet access should feel selective.==================================================4. CLOSET OWNER EXPERIENCE==================================================Closet owners need a very simple private interface.This should NOT feel like complex inventory-management software.The relationship with Poda is the primary product.The interface should simply make the relationship easier.A closet owner should be able to:1. See the items currently associated with their Poda closet.2. Add an item.3. Edit basic item details.4. Mark an item as AVAILABLE or NOT AVAILABLE.5. See whether Poda has selected an available item.6. See whether an item is currently live.7. See whether an item has sold.Basic statuses:CLOSETThe item exists in the user's closet but is not currently offered for sale.AVAILABLEThe closet owner is willing to sell the item.SELECTEDPoda has chosen the item for an upcoming drop.LIVEThe item is currently available in a Poda drop/store.SOLDThe item has sold.Potentially add PAID later, but do not overcomplicate this now.==================================================5. CLOSET ITEM FIELDS==================================================Each item should have basic data such as:- Item ID- Closet owner- Brand- Item name / short description- Category- Size- Condition- Photos- Notes- Owner minimum payout / amount they would be comfortable receiving- Availability status- Poda status- Drop number- Poda list price- Sold price- Poda commission- Owner payoutDo not expose financial/admin fields publicly.Admin should have access to more information than the closet owner or public customer.==================================================6. DROP SYSTEM==================================================Poda should operate around recurring curated drops.The initial intended cadence is approximately every two weeks.Do NOT build artificial Supreme-style scarcity mechanics.The value of the drop comes from:- Poda's curation- limited one-of-one inventory- the quality of the closets- consistent merchandising- giving customers a reason to returnA working weekly/drop flow could be:MONDAY / TUESDAYCloset owners update which pieces they are currently willing to sell.TUESDAY / WEDNESDAYAvailability closes for the upcoming drop.WEDNESDAYPoda reviews the available inventory and selects the strongest pieces.SELECTED pieces are then physically obtained by Poda before being sold publicly.THURSDAYPoda verifies:- condition- measurements- photography- authenticity concerns- pricing- product descriptionsFRIDAYThe curated drop goes live.Initial drops should be SMALL.A first drop may only contain:6–10 pieces.Do not optimize for having a huge store.We would rather have:8 pieces / 5 sellthan:30 pieces / 5 sell.Poda should train customers to trust that everything included in a drop was intentionally selected.==================================================7. PHYSICAL POSSESSION RULE==================================================Closet owners should NOT send their entire closets to Poda.The inventory stays with the closet owner while it is only:CLOSETorAVAILABLEOnce Poda selects an item:AVAILABLE→ SELECTEDPoda arranges to receive the item physically before it becomes:LIVEThis protects Poda from:- incorrect condition descriptions- inaccurate measurements- double-selling- slow consignor shipping- missing items- buyer complaints- poor photography- avoidable authenticity problemsOnly selected items need to move physically to Poda.==================================================8. MARKET NOTES==================================================Market Notes remains an important part of Poda.However, Market Notes should no longer feel like an unrelated fashion newsletter.Market Notes is:Poda's public buying / selling / market brain.There are three primary editorial categories:BUYSELLWATCH--------------------------------------------------BUY--------------------------------------------------What Poda believes is currently worth buying.Examples:- Three designer shirts worth buying under $200- Why this Prada Sport jacket makes sense at $280- What old Armani is actually worth paying for- Five shoes Poda would buy instead of Margiela GATs- Categories that appear underpriced--------------------------------------------------SELL--------------------------------------------------Understanding resale, value and what is worth moving.Examples:- What makes a piece worth accepting on consignment- Why some pieces sell immediately while others sit- What actually makes clothing hold value- What resale buyers reject- When it makes sense to sell something rather than keep it--------------------------------------------------WATCH--------------------------------------------------Things Poda believes are becoming more interesting.Examples:- Brands appearing more frequently in strong stores- Customer demand beginning to shift toward a silhouette- Categories gaining resale momentum- Products store owners say people are requesting- Things Poda is noticing through field research==================================================10. MARKET NOTES RESEARCH ENGINES==================================================Market Notes should be built from four primary sources:PEOPLEInterviews and conversations with:- store owners- buyers- collectors- vintage dealers- fashion employees- merchandisers- independent sellers- stylists- other industry operatorsPRICESMarketplace research:- sold comps- active listings- pricing differences- stale inventory- resale behavior- relative valuePLACESField research:- consignment stores- vintage stores- boutiques- department stores- pop-ups- retail environments- merchandising observationsCUSTOMERSEventually Poda's own data:- what sells- what gets clicked- what people request- what consignors make available- what gets selected- what sits- what sizes/categories moveShort version:PEOPLE + PRICES + PLACES + CUSTOMERS==================================================11. INTERVIEW SYSTEM==================================================Do NOT structure research so that every Market Note requires 3–5 new interviews.Poda is beginning with cold outreach.Each conversation should therefore generate reusable research.One conversation with a store owner could cover:BUY- What are you buying more of?- What feels undervalued?- What gives you confidence an item will move?SELL- What makes you reject an item?- What is difficult to sell?- What matters most when pricing?WATCH- What are customers requesting?- What brands/categories are increasing in demand?- What has surprised you recently?Insights from interviews should be stored and tagged:BUYSELLWATCHOne interview can therefore contribute to several future Market Notes.The interviews themselves do not need to become "Interview with X" articles.Instead, interview insights become primary-source evidence inside Poda's larger point of view.==================================================12. HOW MARKET NOTES CONNECTS TO COMMERCE==================================================Market Notes should not exist just to get views.The loop is:RESEARCH→ PODA FORMS AN OPINION→ MARKET NOTE→ BETTER MERCHANDISING / BUYING DECISION→ DROP→ TRANSACTION→ NEW DATA→ BETTER RESEARCHArticles can naturally lead readers toward:SHOP THE DROPSELL THROUGH PODABut do not force aggressive calls to action into every article.Market Notes should feel credible and editorial first.==================================================13.
